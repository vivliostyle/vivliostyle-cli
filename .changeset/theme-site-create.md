Choose themes with theme.vivliostyle.org in `vivliostyle create`: the theme list comes from the site, which leaves out deprecated, invalid, and moderated packages, and falls back to the npm search; `Choose a theme in the browser` opens the site and receives the choice through a loopback server; a hand-typed theme hidden on the site is confirmed before use. The site URL can be overridden with `VIVLIOSTYLE_THEME_SITE_URL`.
